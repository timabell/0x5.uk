Lately I've been using Obsidian to organise my tasks. I am a fan of David Allen's excellent "Getting Things Done (GTD®)" methodology for orgnising life's infinite demands on one's time, and is it is tool agnostic I have been able to move my setup from Trello to Obsidian (via Logseq where I never got anywhere with GTD).

I've taken the time to publish an 11 minute video showing how I'm using it in a way that is already working pretty well for me.

[![youtube vid screenshot](/images/blog/obsidian-gtd-youtube-thumbnail.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CrQIAxtMv9M)

In the vid I show some basic use of task checkboxes, and then expand on that with queries across markdown files to pull together a persistent homepage in the style of GTD that gives me view of all my projects, actions and inbox.

I've been [practicing my jump-cut editing](https://mastodon.social/@tim_abell/117253885161847399) in [kdenlive](https://apps.kde.org/kdenlive/) to avoid making anyone endure pondering moments and second attempts, let me know what you think.

You can find the [demo vault used in the video on github](https://github.com/timabell/obsidian-demo-vault), including the [homepage file source code](https://github.com/timabell/obsidian-demo-vault/blob/5b13521898356f63b45ffa7b08aa8950d87d16d5/demo-vault/homepage.md?plain=1).
